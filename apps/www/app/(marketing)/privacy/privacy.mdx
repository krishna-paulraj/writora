# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This policy describes what data Writora collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the hosted Writora service. Self-hosted Writora instances are operated by their own administrators, who are responsible for how those instances handle data.

## Data we collect

**Account data.** When you sign up we store your email address, your name, and a hashed password (or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google).

**Content you author.** Articles, drafts, site settings, themes, and images you create or upload are stored so we can host and serve them. Published content is public by design.

**Subscriber emails.** If readers subscribe to your blog, their email addresses are stored on your behalf. Subscriptions use double opt-in, and every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You control this list; we do not use it for our own purposes.

**Payment data.** Payments are processed by Stripe. We store your subscription status and plan; we never store your card number.

**Analytics.** We record page views on blogs to power the analytics dashboard. Views are deduplicated by IP address; we do not build cross-site profiles of readers.

**Cookies.** We use a single JWT authentication cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

## How we use your data

- To provide the service: hosting your sites, generating content you request, sending your newsletters, and showing you analytics.
- To bill you for paid plans.
- To respond when you contact us.
- To send transactional email such as email verification and password resets.

We do not sell your data.

## Subprocessors

We share data with these providers only as needed to run the service:

- **Stripe** — payment processing and subscription billing.
- **OpenAI** — article and image generation. Prompts derived from your requests are sent to OpenAI to produce drafts.
- **Resend** — transactional email and newsletter delivery.
- **DataForSEO** — keyword lookups. Your search terms are sent to DataForSEO to retrieve volume and difficulty data.
- **S3-compatible object storage** — hosting of uploaded and generated images.

## Your rights

You can export or delete your data at any time — send a request through the [contact form](/contact) and we will handle it. Deleting your account removes your sites, content, and subscriber lists.

## Self-hosted instances

If you run Writora on your own infrastructure, your data lives in your own database and this policy does not apply — the operator of that instance is the data controller.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
